Owlery connects to NetSuite through the SuiteTalk REST Record API and SuiteQL. The integration can bring order and item data into Owlery, then write selected fulfillment, receipt, shipment, and freight updates back to NetSuite.

The integration is configuration-driven. Owlery can adapt to your subsidiaries, custom fields, statuses, item types, addresses, units of measure, lot requirements, and operating workflows without asking you to replace the way your team uses NetSuite.

**Where to find it:** `Settings → Integrations`

<Info>
  Owlery leads the integration design. Your team provides access, identifies the workflows that matter, and reviews representative transactions. We handle the mapping, configuration, edge cases, validation, and rollout.
</Info>

## How the integration works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect securely">
    Owlery authenticates with NetSuite token-based OAuth 1.0. Requests are signed with HMAC-SHA256 and sent to the SuiteTalk endpoint for your NetSuite account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover your configuration">
    Owlery can read account metadata such as classifications, departments, locations, cost categories, units of measure, and item records. We use that metadata to configure mappings against your actual NetSuite values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Synchronize orders and items">
    Owlery retrieves new and changed records through paginated, incremental syncs. Each NetSuite record is converted into the facilities, stops, dates, items, references, and statuses used by your Owlery workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply your business rules">
    Rules decide which orders to include, how to route subsidiaries, which fields supply dates and addresses, how statuses map, and how item and packaging data should be interpreted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write back selected events">
    If enabled, Owlery can transform NetSuite orders into item fulfillments or receipts and update shipment fields when freight is booked, picked up, or delivered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and reconcile">
    Owlery paces requests against NetSuite governance limits, retries safe concurrency rejections, reports failures, and preserves stable record identities during re-syncs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Authentication and access

To connect, Owlery needs the following token-based authentication values from your NetSuite administrator:

- **Account ID / Realm**
- **Consumer Key**
- **Consumer Secret**
- **Access Token**
- **Token Secret**

The NetSuite role attached to the token needs permission to read the record types you want to synchronize. It also needs write permission for any fulfillment, receipt, or order-field updates you enable.

During connection testing, Owlery verifies access to purchase orders, sales orders, and transfer orders separately. This makes missing permissions easier to identify before synchronization begins.

<Note>
  Optional features may require access to supporting records such as locations, employees, classifications, departments, cost categories, inventory numbers, and units of measure. Owlery will identify the permissions needed for your selected workflow.
</Note>

## Data synchronized from NetSuite

### Orders

Owlery supports three primary NetSuite transaction families:

| NetSuite record | How Owlery uses it |
| --- | --- |
| Sales Order | Creates a customer shipment with its origin, destination, dates, references, and line items |
| Purchase Order | Creates a supplier shipment and can identify outsourced purchase-order lines |
| Transfer Order | Creates an internal inventory movement between origin and destination facilities |

Depending on the record and your configuration, Owlery can synchronize:

- Internal ID, transaction number, external ID, and reference numbers
- NetSuite status and a configurable Owlery status
- Created and last-modified timestamps
- Creator, employee, or sales representative email
- Customer, vendor, subsidiary, classification, department, and sales channel
- Pickup and delivery facilities, addresses, and dates
- Header notes and line-level handling notes
- Line number, item, SKU, quantity, fulfilled or received quantity, unit, rate, and amount
- Lot or inventory assignments and expiration dates
- Ship method and other configured custom fields

### Item master

Owlery can synchronize inventory, assembly, kit, non-inventory, service, shipping, and other charge item records. The default configuration recognizes more than 15 operational item types, and the included record types can be customized for your account.

Item mapping can include:

- SKU, name, description, and NetSuite record type
- Base and transaction units of measure
- Item weight and weight unit
- Case, carton, each, and pallet packaging relationships
- Cases per pallet, including `TI × HI` calculations where applicable
- Temperature ranges for configured refrigerated products
- Lot numbers and expiration dates

## Addresses and facilities

Addresses are often one of the most account-specific parts of a NetSuite implementation. Owlery can resolve facilities from:

- The shipping address stored directly on an order
- A NetSuite Location record
- A vendor or customer address-book entry
- A configured custom body field
- A `<br>`-delimited address stored in a custom field
- A linked custom location record

Owlery can apply different address sources to sales, purchase, and transfer orders. If the preferred source is missing or incomplete, the integration can use configured fallbacks while preserving the identity of the facility.

Invalid custom addresses are reported for review instead of being silently accepted as complete locations.

## Status, classification, and department rules

Owlery includes default status mappings for purchase, sales, and transfer orders. You can override a mapping when your NetSuite process interprets a standard status differently.

Classification and department rules support NetSuite hierarchies. A rule can match the value directly or match one of its parent records. This lets you apply one rule to an entire branch of your NetSuite structure instead of configuring every child value individually.

For example, one parent classification can identify parcel orders while unmatched classifications remain freight orders.

## Subsidiaries and multiple organizations

A single NetSuite connection can route orders to different Owlery child organizations using the NetSuite subsidiary ID. Owlery validates that every configured destination belongs to the same organization family, preventing records from being routed outside the authorized account structure.

Incremental synchronization also checks the complete organization family. This prevents child-organization activity from being missed when one parent connection serves multiple subsidiaries.

You can also connect multiple NetSuite accounts when separate credentials or account boundaries are the better fit.

## Lots, units, and packaging

Owlery resolves the transaction unit against the item's NetSuite Units Type record. When NetSuite returns an unavailable or unsupported unit record, Owlery can preserve the line using a safe default and report the condition for review.

For lot-controlled inventory, Owlery can:

- Read issue and receipt inventory numbers
- Preserve expiration dates
- Split one NetSuite line into multiple Owlery item lines when it contains multiple lots
- Recombine those item lines by original NetSuite line during write-back
- Match lots by item and location
- Send explicit inventory assignments only when the NetSuite account requires them

This distinction matters because NetSuite accounts with Inventory Status enabled can require different fulfillment behavior from accounts using lot tracking without Inventory Status.

## Write-back workflows

Write-back is optional and configured as individual strategies. You can enable only the actions your NetSuite process needs.

| Owlery event or workflow | NetSuite action |
| --- | --- |
| Sales order is ready for fulfillment | Transform the Sales Order into an Item Fulfillment |
| Transfer shipment leaves its origin | Transform the Transfer Order into an Item Fulfillment |
| Transfer shipment arrives | Transform the Transfer Order into an Item Receipt |
| Purchase shipment arrives | Transform the Purchase Order into an Item Receipt |
| Load is picked up | Write the Owlery load number to configured transaction lines |
| Load is delivered | Write the delivered timestamp to the source transaction |
| Freight is booked | Mark the source transaction as freight booked |
| Item receipt is created | Add allocated freight as landed cost when configured |

Fulfillment and receipt payloads can include line quantities, item and header locations, tracking numbers, shipment dates, transaction dates, and lot assignments. Owlery reads the original NetSuite lines before writing back so the update uses the correct NetSuite line and item identities.

### Landed-cost allocation

When enabled, Owlery can post the booked freight cost to a NetSuite Item Receipt. You choose a NetSuite cost category and one of three allocation methods:

- **Weight** — distributes cost according to line weight, normalizing mixed weight units first
- **Quantity** — distributes cost according to the received quantity
- **Value** — distributes cost according to extended line value

Owlery reconciles rounding so the allocated line amounts equal the total freight cost exactly. If NetSuite has already created the receipt, Owlery can locate the existing receipt and update its landed-cost fields instead.

## Synchronization and reliability

Owlery is designed around NetSuite's API behavior and governance limits:

- **Incremental synchronization** retrieves new and modified records instead of repeatedly importing the full account.
- **Pagination** keeps large record sets manageable.
- **Request pacing** limits concurrent calls and spaces requests to reduce governance conflicts.
- **Targeted backoff** retries requests blocked by NetSuite's concurrency limit with exponential backoff and jitter.
- **Safe write retries** only retry concurrency rejections that NetSuite blocked before processing. Validation and authentication failures are not blindly retried.
- **Stable reconciliation** preserves child-order identities, soft-deletes records that no longer meet configured rules, and restores them if they become eligible again.
- **Reference lookup** lets an authorized user find and intentionally import an exact NetSuite transaction outside the normal background filter.
- **Structured errors** preserve useful NetSuite details for troubleshooting while presenting actionable guidance to your team.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do we need to change our NetSuite processes?">
    Usually, no. Owlery starts by mapping your existing records, fields, statuses, and workflows. If an optional custom field would make selection or write-back clearer, we will explain the benefit and the smallest change required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Owlery work with custom NetSuite fields?">
    Yes. Custom body and line fields can supply dates, addresses, SKUs, notes, sales channels, order-selection flags, and shipment splits. Owlery can also write agreed shipment values to supported custom fields.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we start with read-only synchronization?">
    Yes. Inbound order and item synchronization can be enabled without write-back. Individual write-back strategies can be introduced after your team validates the inbound data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can we backfill historical or excluded orders?">
    Yes. Owlery supports full re-syncs and exact-reference lookups. When an exact order is outside the normal selection rules, Owlery can require explicit confirmation before importing it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can one connection support multiple subsidiaries?">
    Yes. One connection can route NetSuite subsidiaries into the appropriate Owlery child organizations. Separate NetSuite connections are also supported when account or permission boundaries require them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who maintains the mapping after go-live?">
    Owlery owns the integration configuration and works with your designated contact when NetSuite fields, roles, subsidiaries, or workflows change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
