Owlery has an inbound mapping foundation for Cin7 Omni sales and purchase orders. It is designed to translate freight-relevant Cin7 Omni order, contact, address, and line-item data into Owlery.

<Warning>
  The Cin7 Omni connector is not currently available as a standard self-service connection. Authentication and production enablement are scoped with Owlery before rollout. Contact [support@owlery.ai](mailto:support@owlery.ai) to review availability for your account.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Cin7 Omni and Cin7 Core, formerly DEAR Inventory, are separate products. They use different APIs, credentials, records, and Owlery connectors. See the [Cin7 Core guide](/shippers/cin7-core) if your account was previously known as DEAR.
</Info>

## Current integration scope

The current Cin7 Omni implementation includes mappings for:

| Cin7 Omni record | Current Owlery mapping |
| --- | --- |
| Sales Order | Customer shipment with a delivery stop, customer contact, order status, notes, and line items |
| Purchase Order | Supplier shipment with a pickup stop, supplier contact, order status, notes, and line items |

The mapped order data can include:

- Cin7 Omni order ID as the source identity and Owlery order number
- Created and modified timestamps
- Cin7 Omni source status and its corresponding Owlery status
- Delivery instructions
- Customer or supplier identity and contact details
- Billing address used for the current pickup or delivery mapping
- Item name and line comments
- Expected and shipped quantity
- Unit price
- Barcode mapped as the current lot-code value

The mapping recognizes `Draft`, `Submitted`, `Confirmed`, `Cancelled`, and `Closed` order states. Other values remain available as their source status and require review before a production rollout.

## Synchronization design

The connector foundation is designed to:

- Retrieve sales and purchase orders changed since the last successful synchronization
- Request paginated Cin7 Omni order lists
- Pace requests against the documented minute and daily API allowances
- Resolve missing city, state, or country values from a postal code when possible
- Preserve the source record ID and modification timestamp for later synchronization

Current rate controls are designed around 60 requests per minute and 5,000 requests per day.

<Note>
  These capabilities describe the implemented mapping foundation. They do not indicate that authentication, production synchronization, item-master synchronization, custom-field mapping, transfer orders, or write-back are generally available for Cin7 Omni today.
</Note>

## Configuration and discovery

Cin7 Omni implementations must be scoped before connection. Owlery reviews your account requirements to determine whether the current foundation is sufficient or needs additional work.

The design review covers:

| Area | What Owlery evaluates |
| --- | --- |
| Authentication | The supported Cin7 Omni authorization method and credentials for your account |
| Order scope | Whether sales orders, purchase orders, or both should enter Owlery |
| Record selection | Which statuses, branches, sources, or other account values identify freight-relevant orders |
| Address mapping | Whether billing, delivery, branch, or another address should supply each freight stop |
| Order identity | Whether the Cin7 Omni ID, reference, customer order number, or another field should be customer-facing |
| Item mapping | Which product code, barcode, quantity, and packaging fields should populate Owlery items |
| Custom data | Whether Cin7 Omni custom fields are needed for dates, facilities, notes, references, or filtering |
| Outbound events | Whether your workflow requires updates from Owlery to Cin7 Omni; write-back is not part of the current standard scope |

Unlike the production Cin7 Core connector, Cin7 Omni does not currently expose a standard set of customer-configurable settings in Owlery.

## What Owlery needs from you

Because this connector requires a scoped enablement, Owlery first confirms technical availability and the account's authentication requirements. Once secure read access is available, we can inspect representative records and propose the detailed mapping.

Your team confirms that the recommendation matches the intended business outcome. Owlery leads:

- Technical fit and API-access review
- Representative-record discovery after access is established
- Inbound field and status mapping
- Address and item interpretation
- Exception analysis
- Validation and rollout planning

Any capability beyond the current sales and purchase inbound foundation is identified explicitly in the implementation scope before work begins.

## Implementation process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm product and availability">
    Owlery confirms that your system is Cin7 Omni, not Cin7 Core, and reviews the current connector availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validate API access">
    We determine the supported authorization method and confirm that the required order records can be read safely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discover representative records">
    After access is established, Owlery reviews sales and purchase records, statuses, addresses, contacts, line items, and exceptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recommend the mapping">
    We document the smallest inbound design that supports your workflow and identify any capabilities that require additional implementation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customer confirmation">
    Your team confirms that the proposed mapping and scope produce the intended business outcome before rollout.
  </Step>
</Steps>
