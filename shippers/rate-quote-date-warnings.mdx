When a carrier's estimated transit date does not fit the dates requested on your order, Owlery shows a calendar warning beside the rate. The warning is informational, so you can still select the rate after reviewing the dates.

## How Owlery compares dates

Owlery compares the response to the matching order stop by stop number and stop type.

Owlery shows dates in one of these ways:

- A single date appears as one exact date, such as `July 10`.
- A span of dates appears as a range, such as `July 10 – July 12`.
- If no estimated date is shown for a stop, Owlery does not show a warning for that stop because there is no estimate to compare.

If the dates shown are in reverse order, Owlery puts them in chronological order before comparing them.

The ordered dates become the allowed range. Owlery then selects the relevant date from the estimate:

- **Pickup** uses the first date shown in the estimated range.
- **Dropoff** uses the last date shown in the estimated range.

The selected estimated date must fall on or between the ordered dates to avoid a warning. The comparison is inclusive, so matching the first or last ordered date is valid.

## Examples

| Stop | Ordered date shown | Estimated date shown | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pickup | July 10 | July 10 – July 11 | No warning: pickup uses July 10. |
| Pickup | July 10 | July 11 – July 12 | Warning: pickup is estimated for July 11. |
| Dropoff | July 10 – July 12 | July 9 – July 12 | No warning: dropoff uses July 12. |
| Dropoff | July 10 – July 12 | July 9 – July 13 | Warning: dropoff is estimated for July 13. |
| Pickup | July 10 | July 10 – July 11 | No warning: the ordered pickup date is exactly July 10. |
| Dropoff | July 12 | July 10 – July 12 | No warning: the ordered dropoff date is exactly July 12. |

## Where dates appear

In the response list, the **Transit** column shows the pickup date, an arrow, and the dropoff date. It also shows the business-day transit estimate. When the response provides a range of business days, the dropoff side is shown as a date range.

Open a response to see the **Estimated Dates** table. It lists each stop as `Stop N of M`, its type, and the formatted estimated date or date range.

## Find the affected stop

Hover over the calendar warning beside the rate. The tooltip identifies the stop number and type and shows both the estimated and ordered dates.

If the dates do not work for your shipment, review the affected order stop or choose another response with dates that fit the requested range.
