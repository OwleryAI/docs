When a carrier's estimated transit date does not fit the dates requested on your order, Owlery shows a calendar warning beside the rate. The warning is informational, so you can still select the rate after reviewing the dates.

## How Owlery compares dates

Owlery compares the response to the matching order stop by stop number and stop type.

Before comparing, Owlery interprets each date pair using these rules:

- If only one boundary is present, the present date is treated as an exact date. For example, `July 10–(missing)` means exactly July 10.
- If the start and end dates are the same, the pair is treated as an exact date.
- If the dates arrive in reverse order, Owlery normalizes them chronologically.
- If a pair has no valid date, Owlery does not show a warning because there is nothing reliable to compare.

The ordered dates become the allowed range. Owlery then selects the relevant estimated date:

- **Pickup** uses the estimated start date, or the earliest estimated date.
- **Dropoff** uses the estimated end date, or the latest estimated date.

The selected estimated date must fall on or between the ordered dates to avoid a warning.

## Examples

| Stop | Ordered dates | Estimated dates | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pickup | July 10 | July 10–11 | No warning: pickup uses July 10. |
| Pickup | July 10 | July 11–12 | Warning: pickup is estimated for July 11. |
| Dropoff | July 10–12 | July 9–12 | No warning: dropoff uses July 12. |
| Dropoff | July 10–12 | July 9–13 | Warning: dropoff is estimated for July 13. |
| Pickup | July 10–(missing) | July 10–11 | No warning: the ordered pickup date is exactly July 10. |
| Dropoff | (missing)–July 12 | July 10–12 | No warning: the ordered dropoff date is exactly July 12. |

## Find the affected stop

Hover over the calendar warning beside the rate. The tooltip identifies the stop number and type and shows both the estimated and ordered dates.

If the dates do not work for your shipment, review the affected order stop or choose another response with dates that fit the requested range.
